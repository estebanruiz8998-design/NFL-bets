## Why

### 1. Jaguars -7 vs Browns — *the consensus "bet it now" of Week 1*

Both the SportsLine model (covers well over 50% of simulations, one of its strongest Week 1 picks) and CBS's early-picks column back Jacksonville, and CBS specifically says **grab the flat -7 before it moves** (it has bounced between -7 and -7.5). The Jaguars won the AFC South at 13–4 with one of the AFC's most dangerous passing attacks; Cleveland brings a possibly rusty Deshaun Watson, rookie receivers still integrating, and a new head coach (Todd Monken) installing an offense on the road. The 40.5 total also tells you the market expects the Browns to struggle to score.

### 2. Broncos +2.5 at Chiefs (Monday Night Football) — *ESPN's panel pick, and consistent with the market's biggest Week 1 overreaction*

Kansas City is favored off a **6–11 season** against the **14–3 defending division champion**. ESPN's betting panel (Bowen, Maldonado, Solak, Walder) recommends Denver — assuming Bo Nix is fully back from his ankle injury, they see the Broncos, now with Jaylen Waddle's catch-and-run speed, walking into Arrowhead and winning outright (+130). The whole line is priced on Mahomes returning to pre-ACL form in his first game back in nine months.
**The catch:** this is also the game most sensitive to summer news. If Mahomes' rehab stumbles, KC's price collapses; if Nix's ankle lingers, Denver's does. Take the +2.5 now only if you're comfortable riding the news cycle; CBS explicitly files this one under "bets to make later."

### 3. Seahawks -3.5 vs Patriots (season opener) — *lay the key number with the champs*

The defending champions at home, where Mike Macdonald's No. 1 defense sacked Drake Maye six times with two picks in the Super Bowl five months ago. Expert lean is Seattle at -3.5, treating 3.5 as the key-number value point. The counter: New England added A.J. Brown and Romeo Doubs specifically to fix what broke in that game, so if anything this number should compress toward -3 — meaning if it ever touches -3, that's the buy signal, and don't lay more than -3.5.

### 4. 49ers +3.5 vs Rams in Melbourne — *the contrarian side of a steamed line*

This opened Rams -2.5 at the schedule release and is now -3.5 by SportsLine consensus — the market piled onto L.A. after the Myles Garrett trade. That move through the key number of 3 is exactly when the other side gets interesting: you're now getting a 12–5 playoff team (which beat no one worse than the eventual champs in January) at +3.5 on a neutral field where **both** teams make the same 17-hour trip to a venue no NFL team has played. First-ever regular season game in Australia = maximum weirdness, which favors the dog and (per one analyst lean) the Under 48.5.

### 5. Eagles vs Commanders — *the value already left; wait*

The SportsLine model liked Eagles -4.5; DraftKings is now dealing **-5.5**. Chasing a modeled -4.5 edge at -5.5 gives most of it back. Only playable if you find -5 or shorter, or if the line drifts back during camp. (Post-A.J. Brown-trade Philadelphia is also a genuinely different offense — treat May numbers skeptically here.)

### 6. Vikings +1.5 at Packers — *watch list only*

Green Bay opens without Micah Parsons (ACL — PUP candidate, "back early in the season" per the GM, mid-October per other reporting). Minnesota's No. 3 defense travels with Kyler Murray (-1000 to start) getting a revenge-tour narrative. +105 on the moneyline with the better defense and the opponent missing its best player is a live-dog profile — but no strong expert consensus backs it yet, so it stays a quarter-unit watch, pending Murray-in-O'Connell's-scheme preseason evidence.

## Passes worth noting

- **Chargers -11.5 vs Cardinals** — biggest spread of Week 1. Double-digit Week 1 spreads with a rookie-ish opponent (Arizona drafted RB Jeremiyah Love No. 3) are classic stay-away: too much unknown for that much chalk.
- **Bucs–Bengals (O/U 50.5)** — highest total of the week. Cincinnati's offseason (Dexter Lawrence, healthy Burrow) is the futures play (see README); no Week 1 edge identified yet.
- **Everything else** — nine Week 1 games sit at a field goal or less. Coin flips in May stay coin flips in July; wait for September information.

## Timing rules for early lines

1. **Bet now** only where the number is the value (Jags -7, SF +3.5, DEN +2.5) — these get worse if you wait and the market confirms.
2. **Wait** where news is the variable (Chiefs–Broncos health, Eagles line drift, Vikings QB tape).
3. Injuries between now and Sept. 9 void any of this — re-check before betting, and again before kickoff.
