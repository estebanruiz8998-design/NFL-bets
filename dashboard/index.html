    Walk-forward backtest · 1999–2025 · nflverse data

    Model & Backtest

    Elo ratings (margin-of-victory, home field, rest, QB changes) + EWMA totals, logistic-calibrated against market lines, betting at −110. Train 2008–2019, untouched validation 2020–2025.

    The honest headline

    This system does not beat NFL closing lines — nothing built on public box-score features does; only ~6% of model/market disagreement is real signal. Validation ROI on its rare STRONG bets is +2.7% on 53 bets: statistically zero. Its real value: calibrated best-bet rankings, the discipline to PASS fairly-priced games, and flagging stale posted lines in-season, where genuine edges live. Two overfitting traps (+5–8% “ROI” in-sample) were caught and removed by the holdout — treat that as a warning about every shiny backtest you see.
