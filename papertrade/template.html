How to read this honestly

CLV measures whether our prices beat the market’s later prices — the standard professional proxy for edge, and the only one available before games are played. It is a noisy, small-sample signal over one summer: a few points of positive CLV is encouraging, not proof; negative CLV is a cheap early warning. Week 1 spread positions get their final CLV against actual closing lines on Sept 9–14, and then real results when the games finish. The futures can’t be graded until winter — for them this page only ever shows price movement.
